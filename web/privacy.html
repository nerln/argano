Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Who is responsible

argano is run by Eugenio Nerelli, an independent developer based in Spain, who is the controller of the data described here. Contact: write to the developer.

Your video

The repair runs entirely in your browser. Your video is never uploaded, stored or seen by us or by anyone else, and nothing from it is ever sent.

What is collected

  • When a page of argano opens: the page address, the website that sent you and any campaign tag in the link.
  • While a file is repaired: that a file was opened, whether a second video was needed, and whether the repair finished or stopped. Each step is counted under the website or campaign tag that brought the visit, and carries nothing from the video: no name, no size, no content.
  • If you answer the question after a repair: whether the video plays.

These counts are received by GoatCounter, which also derives the browser, the operating system and the country from the request. To tell one visit from another it holds your IP address and browser identifier in memory for up to 8 hours, and never writes them to disk. What is kept are totals, which cannot be linked to a person. No cookies are set and no identifier is kept on your device.

If you switch between light and dark, the choice is remembered in your browser under the name argano-theme. It stays on your device and is never sent.

Purpose and legal basis

To know how many people use argano, where they come from and whether its repairs work. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in running and improving a free tool (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Who receives the data

  • GoatCounter (goatcounter.com), operated by Martin Tournoij in Ireland, which counts visits on our behalf as a processor. Its servers are run by Hetzner Online GmbH in Finland and Germany, so the data stays in the European Union.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States), which serves these pages and may log requests under its own privacy statement. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

How long it is kept

Totals are kept for up to 25 months.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object by writing to the contact above, and you can lodge a complaint with the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

Changes

This policy may change. The date at the top shows the version in force.
