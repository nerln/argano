The video won't play after the recording stopped

The battery died, the card filled up, the app crashed or the phone ran out of space. The file is there, it has a size, and nothing will open it.

Repair it now, free

Nothing is uploaded and there is no account. The repair runs in your browser.

Why a full size file will not play

The recorder writes the frames as it goes and the index for last. Stopped early, it leaves a file that is almost all media with nothing that describes it. That is why the size looks right and every player refuses it.

How to repair it

  1. Copy the file to your computer first. A file on a card or a phone is repaired faster and more safely as a local copy.
  2. Open argano and drop it. It tells you what is wrong and how much of the file is media before doing anything.
  3. If it asks for one, add a few seconds of another video shot with the same phone, camera or drone, at the same resolution and frame rate. Then press Repair and save the copy that plays.

What it cannot do

argano reads MP4, MOV and M4V with H.264 or H.265 video and AAC sound. Not MKV, not AVI, not a camera's own raw format. Frames that never reached the card cannot come back.
