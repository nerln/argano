Photo Booth video won't open

The recording shows 0:00, or QuickTime says the file is not compatible. It usually happens to long recordings, and the video is almost always still there.

Repair it now, free

Nothing is uploaded and there is no account. The repair runs in your browser.

What happened to the file

A video file has two parts: the frames, written while you record, and an index that says where each frame starts, written once at the end. When Photo Booth stops before writing that index, the frames are all on disk but no player can find them.

How to repair it

  1. Find the file. Photo Booth keeps its recordings in Pictures, inside Photo Booth Library: right click it in Finder, choose Show Package Contents and open the Pictures folder. Copy the broken video somewhere else before doing anything.
  2. Record three seconds in Photo Booth on the same Mac, at the same size. Photo Booth does not write its settings inside the video, so argano reads them from this short clip.
  3. Open argano, drop the broken file, then the short clip, and press Repair. You get a new copy that plays. Your original is only read.

What it cannot do

If Photo Booth stopped before the frames reached the disk, they are not in the file and no tool can bring them back. argano tells you how much of the file is media before it starts.
